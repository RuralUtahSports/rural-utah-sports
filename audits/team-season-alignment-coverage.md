# Utah football classification and region coverage

Updated November 20, 2026. The database has 139 programs and 6,506 dated school-seasons from 1925 to 2026. All 118 current RUS programs are represented. The other 21 have historical program pages in the Teams directory.

## Coverage

All 77 annual indexes from 1950 through 2026 and all 1,044 linked conference pages were processed. 6,381 school-seasons link directly to a season-specific conference page. Other entries link to evidence such as an official classification publication, football bracket, or historical school schedule. Records contain classification and region facts only. [Annual standings](https://utah-football.com/) · [School archive](https://utah-football.com/f/allteams.htm) · [Official results](https://www.uhsaa.org/yearlyresults/)

**Complete against the reviewed sources** means every indexed conference was reviewed, each participating school was matched, both fields are sourced, and no additional gap was identified in the reviewed game histories or supplemental sources. Future discoveries can change this designation.

- Complete against the reviewed sources (42 years): 1950–1953, 1956–1961, 1963, 1970–1971, 1973–1980, 1983, 1985–1988, 1992, 1998–1999, 2001–2005, 2009, 2011, 2013–2014, 2017–2018, 2022–2023.
- All indexed groups reviewed, with open questions: 1954–1955, 1962, 1964–1969, 1972, 1981–1982, 1984, 1989–1991, 1993–1997, 2000, 2006–2008, 2010, 2012, 2015–2016, 2019–2021, 2024–2026.
- Partial before the annual index: 1925, 1927–1928, 1937–1949. Class or format can often be established for playoff entrants and six-player teams. Complete statewide region rosters were unavailable in the sources reviewed. The remaining earlier years have no dated alignment in this edition.
- Existing rows with a blank classification: 42. Existing rows with a blank region: 111. The CSV also identifies known school-seasons with no alignment, including 1,026 pre-1950 school-seasons from current programs’ RUS game histories. The pre-1950 figures are lower bounds because the current team pages do not enumerate all discontinued programs.

The [verification list](team-season-alignment-gaps.csv) names the program, year, missing information and research lead. The [source counts](team-season-archive-counts.json) give accepted memberships for every conference page. Counts exclude five cross-listed memberships described below. A blank field does not count as a class or region.

## Checks and source decisions

- Verified membership from each annual standings table independently. Compared the results to RUS season histories, RUS class-labeled playoff brackets, and school-specific archive game logs for all 140 archive identities. The Utah School for the Deaf and USDB profiles are represented as one program. USDB describes reinstating football after more than fifty years. [USDB annual report](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/files/1029415.pdf)
- All 115 programs across the 19 archived 2024 groups have both fields. USDB is a 116th assignment. Its official eight-player placement and archived forfeit support a dated record. Its A/B division is unknown. The nine schools ranked in the official eight-player final RPI all appear in the annual standings. [2024 standings](https://utah-football.com/s/2024/fb24.htm) · [UHSAA football alignment](https://uhsaa.org/news/uploads/202325%20Alignment%20Final.pdf) · [Final eight-player RPI](https://www.uhsaa.org/rpi/football/2024/8playerfinal.pdf)
- Escalante’s 2025 class is supported by the official eight-player alignment, a school presentation to the city, and its archived result. Its region is unresolved. Multi-year placement alone does not establish varsity participation for USDB in 2025–26 or Escalante in 2026. [Escalante council minutes](https://escalanteutah.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/CCM-3182025.pdf) · [Escalante results](https://utah-football.com/f/z/escalant_re.htm)
- Five duplicate listings were checked against the conference records and schedules. Box Elder played six Region 1 decisions in 1972 and had a single crossover tie counted under Region 4. Ogden played its seven-game 1977 league schedule in 3A Region 4, and Bonneville its seven-game league schedule in 4A Region 1. The same Ogden–Bonneville game accounted for each school's one entry in 4A Region 2. Delta and North Sanpete were both seeded from 3A Region 8 in 1999. Their game also appears in Region 9. Each team-year has one regular-season alignment. [1972 Region 1](https://utah-football.com/s/1972/4areg1.htm) · [1977 crossover](https://utah-football.com/s/1977/4areg2.htm) · [1999 UHSAA bracket](https://uhsaa.org/Publications/YearlyResults/YearlyResults19992000.pdf)
- The UHSAA football record book uses Class A/B through 1968 and AA/A/B in 1969–70. The conference archive uses 1A/1B and 3A/2A/1A, respectively. This database uses the official era names for those seasons. Beginning in 1971 the official numeric labels apply. In mixed-class leagues the conference page establishes region. A secondary source establishes football class where individual playoff evidence differs from the league heading. [UHSAA football record book](https://www.uhsaa.org/RecordsBook/Records_BookFootball.pdf) · [Championship history](https://utah-football.com/state_champs.htm)
- For example, the 2007–10 Region 9 standings include Dixie, Pine View and Snow Canyon under a 3A heading. Contemporary reports identify them as the league’s 4A schools. In 1993 the RUS 3A bracket abbreviates Richfield as Rich. UHSAA identifies Richfield, so Rich retains its independently verified 1A membership. The 2015–16 RUS bracket shorthand also differs from the 3AA conference labels. [Region 9 alignment](https://www.deseret.com/2008/6/10/20257505/uhsaa-makes-final-decision-on-realignment/) · [1993 UHSAA results](https://www.uhsaa.org/Publications/YearlyResults/YearlyResults19931994.pdf)
- Gunnison Valley and Water Canyon have a separate eight-player playoff classification in 2022. Their year links retain the regular-season standings. The 2025 Rich independent assignment is retained from the original PR’s source. Rich participated in eight-player playoffs. [2022 tournament](https://uhsaa.org/tournaments/2022-23/8FB.pdf) · [Gunnison’s two formats](https://www.deseret.com/2022/10/20/23402751/high-school-football-6a-5a-4a-3a-2a-1a-playoffs-preview/) · [2025 Rich standings](https://utah-football.com/s/2025/indepen.htm)
- Earlier six-player competition and non-UHSAA eight-man competition have distinct labels. A statewide eight-player heading without subdivisions uses the source’s “8-Man” conference label. Independent and Freelance identify the scheduling group. Neither term supplies a classification. Whitehorse and Monument Valley’s 2020 pandemic hiatus is documented separately from their other independent seasons. [Six-player records](https://uhsaa.org/RecordsBook/Records_Book6MF.pdf) · [Private football programs](https://www.deseret.com/2011/8/18/20210217/high-school-football-felt-s-facts-week-1/) · [San Juan Record](https://www.sjrnews.com/news/revisiting-top-stories-remarkable-2021)
- Before 1950, only explicitly labeled school-years were entered. RUS game notes and the UHSAA record book distinguish A/B participants in the 1920s even where the bracket simply says “State”. The football and six-player sections were checked separately from other sports in the record book. Payson’s school history identifies its 1943 Class B, Region III assignment. [Payson school history](https://sites.google.com/nebo.edu/phshalloffame/sports-hall-of-fame/profiles-for-sports-hall-of-fame/teams/1943-football)

## Program identities

The historical directory includes Abundant Life, Alterra, Brigham Young High, Diamond Ranch, Dugway, East Carbon, Escalante, Granite, Green River, Hinckley, Intermountain, North Cache, Notre Dame, Roosevelt, Sorenson’s Ranch, South (Salt Lake City), South Cache, South Rich, Utah Schools for the Deaf and the Blind, Wasatch Academy, Wayne. These are programs outside the 118-team current RUS directory. Inclusion here does not establish that the school itself closed.

The name matches include American Leadership Academy → ALA, Cedar → Cedar City, Grand County → Grand, Monument Valley → Monument Val, UMA Camp Williams → UMA-Lehi, UMA Hill Field → UMA-Hillfield, and Lincoln → Orem as designated in the UHSAA football record book. South (Salt Lake City) and South Sevier are separate. North Cache and South Cache have separate records from Sky View. South Rich and Rich use the archive’s separate program identities, as do Alterra, Roosevelt and Union. The Intermountain entry is tied specifically to the Brigham City archive profile. [Utah State Archives on South Cache](https://archives.utah.gov/research/entities/4504/) · [North Cache history](https://northcache.ccsdut.org/our-school/about-us/school-history) · [Archive school index](https://utah-football.com/f/allteams.htm)
