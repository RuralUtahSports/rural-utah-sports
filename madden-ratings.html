How Madden Ratings work: Game-by-game 2026 points scored and allowed are adjusted for opponent quality and recent performance, with a light historical ELO prior for teams with fewer results. Offense and defense are converted into relative 50–99 percentile grades. Overall blends 51% OFF and 49% DEF, with a small record adjustment. Teams with fewer than four completed in-state games are marked provisional.

Important: Madden OVR, OFF and DEF grades do not calculate point spreads. Only the RUS Power Ratings have the one-rating-point-equals-one-spread-point rule. Both systems are estimates based on game results, not verified scouting assessments of individual players.
