Simulator Rules

Regular SeasonEvery team plays exactly 20 regular-season games against teams in its current classification. Standings are ordered by wins, point differential, points scored and then simulator rating.
ChampionshipsThe top two teams after the 20-game season play a neutral-site championship game. The title game does not change the 20-game standings record.
PromotionIn 5A through 8-Player, first and second place move up automatically. Third and fourth play one game for the final promotion spot.
RelegationThe bottom three teams in 6A through 1A move down one level. No team can be promoted above 6A or relegated below 8-Player.
