Scope
How MVP scores work: Overall production combines the stronger side of the ball with 35% of the other side. Passing TD +5; interception thrown or reported offensive fumble lost −5; defensive interception +10; reported forced fumble or defensive recovery +5. Unknown or conflicting fumble counts are not scored. Team context uses record (50%), opponent-record SOS (30%) and quality wins (20%), with the same limited adjustments as All-Utah. Statewide overall, offense and defense use classification weights (6A 1.18×, 5A 1.14×, 4A 1.08×, 3A 1.00×, 2A 0.95×, 1A 0.90×, 8-Player 0.88×). Classification, region and school-size races use unweighted scores. The overall leader is excluded from the other awards in that scope. Region MVP projections do not change locked All-Region awards.
