Methodology & Sources

This page explains what RUS data means, where it comes from and which numbers are original Rural Utah Sports calculations rather than official association statistics.

Historical Scores & Program Records

RUS compiles historical results from available score archives, public reporting, record books and program research. Sources used across the project include historical research associated with George Felt, Deseret News reporting and score data, MaxPreps records, UHSAA materials when applicable, and information supplied by players, coaches and local communities.

Historical sources sometimes conflict. RUS favors the most specific and verifiable game-level record available and accepts documented corrections.

Current Scores, Rosters & Statistics

Current-season pages use the latest data files available to RUS. Scoreboard and game pages may combine RUS-maintained game records with Deseret News game information when available. Player pages and leaderboards are built from the current roster/stat data loaded into the RUS database.

A visible “Data updated” stamp appears on major data pages. That timestamp reflects the newest primary data file used by the page, not necessarily the kickoff time or publication time of the underlying source.

RUS Rankings

Rankings labeled RUS are Rural Utah Sports rankings. They are not official UHSAA rankings unless a page explicitly says otherwise. Current rankings may incorporate results, team strength and RUS analysis.

ELO Ratings

RUS ELO is a power-rating system that changes after games based on the relative strength of the two teams and the result. Higher-rated teams are expected to win more often. ELO is useful for comparing team strength across schedules, but it is a model, not an official ranking.

Expected win probability = 1 / (1 + 10^((Opponent ELO − Team ELO) / 400))

RUS Line / Projected Margin

The RUS Line converts the model’s projected score difference into a simple projected margin. For example, “Team A -14” means the RUS model projects Team A to win by 14 points.

RUS Lines are analytical projections only. They are not sportsbook odds, wagering advice or betting lines.

RPI / Playoff Picture

Pages labeled provisional or projected use the formula and assumptions described on that page. When RUS recreates an association formula, the page identifies it as a projection and notes any data that is estimated, unavailable or temporarily assigned a default value.

Scorigami

RUS Scorigami means a final score combination that has not previously appeared in the RUS Utah high school football game database. It is based on the games currently contained in that database, so a newly discovered historical game can change a Scorigami result.

Corrections & Transparency

RUS is a living historical database. When a score, player, school name, record or other entry appears wrong, use the Report an Error tool and include a source when possible. Corrections are reviewed against available records before the site data is changed.
