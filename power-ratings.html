RUS Power Ratings

Every Power Rating point equals one point on a neutral-field projected spread. If Team A rates 88 PWR and Team B rates 51 PWR, Team A is favored by 37. These opponent-adjusted ratings are separate from the Madden-style OVR, OFF and DEF grades.

Method: Current-season completed Utah-vs-Utah game margins adjusted for opponents, with recent games weighted more, plus a small ELO prior. Blowouts are capped at 55 for fitting. The 0–99 ratings are uniformly compressed only if the full performance range exceeds 99 points; the displayed rating difference is always the displayed neutral-field spread. This is a projection, not a guaranteed final margin. Games with contradictory winner/score data are excluded, and 8-player results are kept separate.
